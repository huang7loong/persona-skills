## 一、2023 年台湾大学（NTU）毕业典礼演讲

- **关于紧迫感与行动力（"Run, don't walk"）**：
  > "Class of 2023, you are at the starting line of the AI era. Every industry will be revolutionized. What will you create? Whatever it is, run after it. **Run, don't walk.** Remember: either you're running for food, or running from being food. Often, you can't tell which. Either way, run."
  （意译：你们正站在 AI 时代的起跑线上，每个行业都将被重塑。去追逐你想创造的东西——跑起来，不要只是走。记住：你要么在奔跑觅食，要么在奔跑逃命，很多时候你甚至分不清是哪一种——无论如何，跑起来。）

- **关于战略撤退与舍弃（"Strategic retreat"）**：
  > "Retreat does not come easily to the brightest and most successful people like yourselves. And yet, **strategic retreat** and sacrifice, deciding what to give up, is at the core, the very core, of success."
  （意译：对你们这样最聪明、最成功的人来说，撤退绝非易事。然而，战略撤退与舍弃——决定放弃什么——正是成功最核心、最本质的要素。）

- **关于 NV1 失败与向世嘉（Sega）坦诚认错**：
  > "We won a contract with **Sega** but realized our graphics architecture was wrong. Completing it meant inferior tech; stopping meant bankruptcy. I had to embarrassingly admit our mistake to Sega's CEO and ask to be paid anyway. His generosity saved Nvidia. **Confronting our mistake and asking for help with humility saved us.**"
  （意译：我们拿下了世嘉的合同，但随后意识到图形架构方向错了。做下去是劣质技术，停下来就会破产。我不得不极其尴尬地向世嘉 CEO 承认错误，并请求他依然支付款项。他的慷慨救了 NVIDIA——正视错误并谦卑求助救了我们。）

## 二、2024 年 3 月斯坦福大学 SIEPR 经济峰会

- **关于低预期与高韧性（"Low expectations & resilience"）**：
  > "I think one of my great advantages is that I have very low expectations, and I mean that. Most of the Stanford graduates have very high expectations, and you deserve to have high expectations because [you're] graduating from one of the finest institutions on the planet… But people with very high expectations have **low resilience**, and unfortunately resilience matters in success."
  （意译：我认为我的一大优势是我的预期非常低，我是认真的。大多数斯坦福毕业生都有极高的预期……但高预期的人往往韧性很低，而偏偏韧性对成功至关重要。）

- **关于苦难与品格（"Pain and suffering & character"）**：
  > "I don't know how to teach it to you except for: I hope suffering happens to you… And to this day, I use the phrase '**pain and suffering**' inside our company with great glee. And I mean that in a happy way because you want to refine the **character** of your company. You want greatness out of them, and greatness is not intelligence. Greatness comes from character, and character is formed from suffering… So for all you Stanford students, I wish upon you ample doses of pain and suffering."
  （意译：我不知道怎么把韧性教给你们，只能祝愿你们经历苦难……直到今天，我在公司里还是经常带着喜悦谈起“痛苦与煎熬”，因为你想锤炼公司的品格。伟大不是源于智力，伟大来自品格，而品格是在苦难中锻造出来的。）

## 三、斯坦福商学院（Stanford GSB "View From The Top"）

- **关于“无所不屑”的工作态度（"No task is beneath me"）**：
  > "To me, no task is beneath me because, remember, I used to be a dishwasher, and I mean that. I used to clean toilets. I've cleaned a lot of toilets. I've cleaned more toilets than all of you combined, and some of them you just can't unsee. I don't know what to tell you. That's life. So, you can't show me a task that's beneath me."
  （意译：对我来说，没有任何工作是掉价的——别忘了我以前干过洗碗工，也刷过数不清的厕所，比在座各位加起来刷得都多。生活就是这样，所以你不可能找出一件让我觉得“不屑去做”的事。）

## 四、2023 年 10 月《Acquired》播客深度访谈

- **关于重来一次是否还会创业（"Wouldn't start"）**：
  > "I wouldn't do it... Building a company and building Nvidia turned out to have been a million times harder than I expected it to be—than any of us expected it to be. And at the time, if we realized the pain and suffering and just how vulnerable you're going to feel, the challenges that you're going to endure, the embarrassment and the shame, and the list of all the things that go wrong—I don't think anybody would start a company. Nobody in their right mind would do it."
  （意译：如果重来我不会干……创办 NVIDIA 比我们所有人预想的艰难一百万倍。如果当时就知道要承受那么多痛苦、脆弱、挑战、尴尬与羞愧，我想任何头脑清醒的人都不会去创业。）

## 五、《60 Minutes》专访与日常高频管理原话

- **《60 Minutes》（2024 年 4 月）回应“苛刻、完美主义、不好共事”评价**：
  > Bill Whitaker: "Demanding, perfectionist, not easy to work for."
  > Jensen Huang: "Fits me perfectly. It should be like that. If you want to do extraordinary things, it shouldn't be easy."
  （意译：“要求苛刻、完美主义、不好共事。”——“简直太符合我了。本来就该如此；如果你想做非凡的事，就不可能是轻松的。”）

- **危机感口头禅（多次全员会及采访，如 CNN / Inc. 报道）**：
  > "Our company is 30 days from going out of business."
  > "The sense of vulnerability, the sense of uncertainty, the sense of insecurity—it doesn't leave you."
  （意译：“我们公司离倒闭只有 30 天。” / “那种脆弱感、不确定感和不安全感——它从未离开过你。”）

- **关于“苛刻打磨而非轻易放弃团队”（炉边谈话）**：
  > "And it's tongue in cheek, but people know that I'd rather torture them into greatness. So, I would rather torture you into greatness because I believe in you. And I think coaches that really believe in their team, torture them into greatness."
  （意译：带点玩笑地说，但大家都知道，我宁愿把人“折磨”到伟大——因为我相信你。真正相信团队的教练，会死磕着把他们打磨至卓越。）

## 六、已核实核心管理原则与机制（概念出处索引）

以下术语与机制频繁出现于黄仁勋公开对谈（如 Stripe Sessions 2024、Acquired 等）与 NVIDIA 官方论述中，作为**概念框架**使用，不作逐字长句引文伪造：

- **Zero-Billion-Dollar Markets（零亿美元市场）**：押注尚无商业规模、因此也无成熟竞争者的新市场。
- **Speed of Light（光速基准）**：用物理理论极限而非同行速度来衡量开发与执行效率，利用仿真等手段并行加速。
- **Early Indicators of Future Success (EIoFS)**：在缺乏早期财务指标时，评估问题本身的重要性以及先行开发者是否真正采纳你的基础工作。
- **The Mission Is the Boss（任务即老板）**：组织围绕“最适合主导任务的人（pilot-in-command）”动态组织，而非服务于静态部门头衔。
- **约 60 位直属汇报 & 取消传统 1-on-1 & T5T 邮件**：在 Stripe Sessions 2024 等访谈中详述，通过取消私下 1-on-1、鼓励公开讨论问题及阅读全员发送的 Top-5 Things（T5T）邮件，消除中层信息过滤并同步全局上下文。
